What this is

We opened Open the way a new member does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and revenue across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Open: installed it, walked every screen from the manifesto to the first class, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people before they decide.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the black, the wordmark, the manifesto, your pill buttons and round arrow, your date wheel, the breathing dots, your paywall and press row. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us — we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
