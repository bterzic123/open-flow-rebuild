# Growth Audit — Open

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/open-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for Open: Breathwork + Meditation, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Open's own design. Only the sequence changes — the black, the wordmark, the manifesto, the pill buttons and round arrow, the date wheel, the breathing dots, the paywall and the press row are all Open's.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Let people in before you ask for an account | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Ask what they came for, then show their first week | CR +10–20% |
| 03 | Name their week on the paywall, next to your Editors' Choice | CR +15–20% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications and Health after the trial starts | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Catch "Later" with one second chance | ARPU +10–15% |

Every price, rating, quote, program name and press logo comes from Open's own App Store listing, website and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Open.

## Run locally

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8921
```

Then open http://localhost:8921. Add `?open=proto` to land on the prototype.
